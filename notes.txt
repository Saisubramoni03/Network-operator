and are not cross-referenced. As a result, the organization 
 cannot proactively identify which equipment may be delayed and which upcoming 
 network activities will be affected.

5. Example of the final Synapt answer

User asks:

"Will any equipment shortage affect the Bangalore rollout scheduled next month?"

Synapt could answer:

Yes. The Bangalore rollout is at high risk due to a potential shortage of RF Module X.

Reasoning:

The rollout requires 500 units.
Current available inventory is 120 units.
380 additional units are dependent on PO-1045.
The supplier's historical delivery records show an average delay of approximately 6 weeks for this module.
The planned delivery date is October 20, while the rollout requires the equipment before November 5.
The vendor contract contains a substitution clause that permits an approved alternate module.

Recommended action:

Check availability with the secondary distributor.
Invoke the substitution clause if technically approved.
Escalate PO-1045 with the primary vendor.
Consider expedited shipping before the shortage enters the rollout critical path.

This is much more valuable than simply returning:

"PO-1045 is delayed."
